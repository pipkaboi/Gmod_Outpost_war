[h1]Outpost War v1.4.0[/h1]
Place outposts for different teams and watch NPC squads fight for them. Outposts spawn NPCs in waves: a garrison guards the point, the rest form squads that follow real routes to capture enemy or neutral outposts. Captured outposts switch sides and spawn the captor's NPCs. Join the war yourself and lead a squad.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Any NPC from the spawn menu (HL2 and addons), chosen or default weapon
[*]NPC mix per outpost: several NPC types and weapons with shares (e.g. 70% SMG, 30% shotgun)
[*]Presets (outpost recipes): save, load and share tool settings, reset to defaults
[*]Waves, garrison, squads with leaders (star above the leader), a bigger star above players in the war
[*]Teams 1-10 + neutral outposts, capture zone with progress bar
[*]Navmesh routes: stairs, multiple floors, around walls, fences and ledges
[*]Squads march in a column along the leader's trail and queue on narrow stairs
[*]NPCs fight on their own (weapon range). Compatible with combat AI mods (Combat Intelligence AI etc.): the addon only leads NPCs to points and doesn't interrupt their maneuvers
[*]NPCs open doors and break glass in their way
[*]Players join teams, capture points, respawn at their outposts and lead a squad of free NPCs
[*]Corpse and weapon cleanup, per-outpost NPC limit, HUD, English and Russian
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Q → [b]Outpost War → Outpost Creator[/b]. LMB - place, RMB - remove, R - apply current settings
[*]Place outposts of 2+ teams (or a neutral one)
[*]Join the war: [b]Outpost War → Settings → Player[/b] → choose team → [b]Join team[/b]
[/olist]

[h2]Console commands[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]outpost_war_join <1-10>[/b] - join a team, 0 = leave
[*][b]outpost_war_squad_dismiss[/b] - dismiss your squad
[*][b]outpost_war_clear_npcs[/b] - (admin) remove all outpost NPCs
[*][b]outpost_war_path_reset[/b] - (admin) clear route cache (after nav_generate)
[*][b]outpost_war_log_clear[/b] - (admin) clear debug log
[/list]

[h2]Server settings[/h2]
Also in [b]Outpost War → Settings → Server Settings[/b]. All names start with [b]outpost_war_[/b].
[list]
[*][b]capture_time[/b] 15 - seconds for one NPC to capture
[*][b]native_combat[/b] 1 - NPCs decide when to fight by their own AI
[*][b]engage_dist[/b] 800 - engage distance when native_combat is 0
[*][b]combat_linger[/b] 8 - seconds of combat readiness after losing the enemy
[*][b]outpost_war_reset_settings[/b] (button in Server Settings) - reset all server settings
[*][b]corpse_time[/b] 20 - remove corpses and dropped weapons after N seconds (0 = never)
[*][b]ignore_players[/b] 0 - NPCs ignore players who are not in a team
[*][b]tint[/b] 1 - tint NPCs with team color
[*][b]open_doors[/b] 1, [b]unlock_doors[/b] 0, [b]break_glass[/b] 1
[*][b]player_squad[/b] 4 - max NPCs in a player's squad
[*][b]spawn_at_outpost[/b] 1 - players respawn at their team's outpost
[*][b]debug[/b] 0 - 1 = draw routes, 2 = also log to data/outpost_war_log.txt (needs developer 1)
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]NPCs need a navmesh: [b]sv_cheats 1[/b] + [b]nav_generate[/b] if the map has none
[*]Nextbots can be spawned and capture points, but are not controlled by the addon
[*]VJ Base NPCs are supported: mixed VJ / HL2 teams, VJ allies of players in their team
[*]ignore_players works only for players who are not in a team. To make ALL NPCs ignore everyone (e.g. in singleplayer while watching), use the built-in [b]ai_ignoreplayers 1[/b]
[*]Outposts with no navmesh nearby (roofs) can't be reached; squads pick another target
[/list]
